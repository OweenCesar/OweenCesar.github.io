Prini Chatbot

Prini Bot 🤖 – University Services Chatbot

🚀 A First Step into Team Development:

During my second-semester Internet Technologies course, I worked with a team to develop Prini, a chatbot designed to assist university students with essential services. This was my first experience working on a real project with a team, and it was full of trial and error—but that’s exactly what made it so valuable.

✨ What Prini Does:

✅ Provides information about the menu of the day 🥗

✅ Shares price details 💰

✅ Helps with basic university-related queries 🎓

💻 My Role & Lessons Learned:

I was in charge of the front-end development, where I gained hands-on experience in HTML and CSS. While I focused on the interface, my teammates worked on the back-end and chatbot logic, using Azure for deployment.

This project wasn’t perfect, and I made plenty of mistakes along the way. But thanks to that, I learned some of the most important lessons—especially how crucial communication and a solid strategy are from the very beginning. It showed me that a well-planned approach can make a huge difference in teamwork and project success.

Looking back, Prini was a great starting point, and I’m excited to apply these lessons to future projects! 🚀

Project link: https://prini.azurewebsites.net/
