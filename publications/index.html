University life isn’t just about lectures and exams—it’s also about growth, exploration, and new experiences. This section is a collection of things I’ve done outside the classroom, from earning certifications and participating in university activities to traveling and mentoring new students.

Experiences - Welcoming exchange students from over 100 countries at the TH Deggendorf

Every year, the Deggendorf Institute of Technology welcomes international exchange students who, just like I did in my first semester, arrive eager to explore a new country. Together, we made their first week at the university unforgettable—full of new experiences, friendships, and a warm welcome to their journey ahead.
